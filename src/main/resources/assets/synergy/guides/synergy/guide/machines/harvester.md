# Harvester

A block that harvest and chop stuff

It will automatically export items to the nearest storage placed at one of the face of it else it will drop everything harvested!

What can harvest :
- any crop mono-block
- any tree (oak , birch , spruce , dark oak , jungle , acacia , cherry , mangrove , large spruce , large jungle , nether fungus)
- any tall-crop (sugar canes , cactus , bamboo)
- cocoa beans
- melons and pumpkings
- berries
- any mushrooms (only with grow stage)
- potted azalea (only with grow stage)
- nether warts

Note: It can harvest trees but dont replant the saplings!

Configurator can allow to show AOE of this machine

It require FE to work
